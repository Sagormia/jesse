Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains how Jesse Giles Photography collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit this website or book a photography session.

Information we collect

When you contact us or book a session, we may ask for personal details such as your name, email address, phone number and event information (like the date and location of your shoot). This helps us schedule your session, communicate with you and deliver your photographs.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, plan and photograph your session, deliver your gallery, and keep records required for our business. If you opt in, we may occasionally share news or offers — you can unsubscribe at any time.

Sharing your information

We respect your privacy and will never sell your personal data. We may share information only with trusted third-party providers when necessary — for example, payment processors, online gallery hosts or printing services. We may also share information if required by law or if necessary to protect our legal rights.

Protecting your information

We use reasonable security measures to keep your information safe, including secure servers and up-to-date software. No website or online system is completely secure. If you ever suspect a security issue, please let us know immediately.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you
  • Request corrections if something is wrong
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Withdraw any consent you've given for marketing

To do any of the above, email jessegiles1@gmail.com and we will respond as quickly as possible.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic analytics to understand how visitors find and use it. Any analytics data is used in aggregate and does not identify you personally. Your browser settings let you block or delete cookies at any time.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children online. Photographs of children are taken only with a parent or guardian's permission.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the changes on this page with a new effective date. We encourage you to check back occasionally.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, contact jessegiles1@gmail.com or call (407) 235-5281.
