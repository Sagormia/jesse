05 The experience

Easy from first hello to final gallery.

No guesswork, no chasing, no stress spiral. A clear process so you can actually enjoy being engaged.

  1. 01

    Say hello

    Tell me your date, your venue, and what matters most. I'll reply quickly with pricing, availability, and full galleries so you can see a whole day — not just a highlight reel.

    Inquiry · same-day reply
  2. 02

    Make it yours

    We build a photo timeline around your ceremony, your people, and the light. You get a simple shot list, a venue walkthrough, and honest answers to every question — including the awkward ones.

    Planning · timeline · shot list

    The day itself

    I show up early, licensed and insured, with a second shooter if you've added one. Gentle direction when you need it, invisible when you don't. Your job is to be there. Mine is to catch it.

    Coverage · 4 to 10 hours

    A private online gallery, edited with print rights, ready to download, share and print. Start with the highlights, then settle in for the rest of the story.

08 Good to know

Questions, answered.

Everything couples and families usually ask before booking. If yours isn't here, just ask — I answer my own messages.

Ask Jesse directly →

Start by sending an inquiry with your date and venue. I'll confirm availability, send the full pricing guide and galleries, and we'll hop on a call if you'd like. Your date is held once we have a signed agreement and retainer — I'll walk you through both.

Every collection includes edited high-resolution images with print rights and a private online gallery. You also get planning support before the day, a photo timeline and shot list, and the peace of mind that your photographer is licensed and insured.

Always. I photograph across Central Florida — Winter Park, Celebration, Lake Nona, Winter Garden, St. Cloud, Mount Dora and beyond — plus the rest of Florida and destination weddings. Travel is quoted upfront so there are no surprises.

Yes — a second shooter is $75 per hour with a 4-hour minimum. It's a great fit for larger weddings, two getting-ready locations, or a timeline where you want extra angles without adding time to your day.

Engagement sessions are $250 and family portrait sessions start at $250. They're a lovely way to get comfortable in front of the camera before the wedding — and a good excuse for new photos of the people you love.

Friday and Saturday weddings require a 6-hour minimum, and the Saturday minimum investment is $1,200. Friday dates start at Package C as well. Weekday elopements and intimate ceremonies can be tailored to a shorter timeline.

Thank you for your service. First responder and military discounts are available, and there's a discount for paying in full. Mention either when you inquire and I'll include the details in your quote.

We make a plan and then we make the best of it. Florida weather is part of the story — I watch the forecast all week, scout covered options at your venue, and keep clear umbrellas in the car. Some of the most romantic photographs happen in the rain.

Through a private online gallery you can download, share with family and print from — print rights included. Edited images are delivered within 1–3 weeks of your session, and I'll send a first look at the highlights while the full gallery is finished.
